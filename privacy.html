Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What Shipped uses the YouTube Data API and the Instagram Graph API only to upload its own daily videos to its own YouTube channel (@whatshipped) and Instagram account (@whatshipped.ai).

  • We do not collect, store or share personal data of any other user.
  • The only account the app accesses is the What Shipped channel owner's own account, with the upload permissions they granted.
  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to publish What Shipped videos.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in Meta's Business Integrations settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. This app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
